# PWA alignment with the IndiaFOSS 2026 landing page

Reference: [official IndiaFOSS 2026](https://fossunited.org/indiafoss/2026),
captured 8 September 2026 in Chromium at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844.

[Open the side-by-side screenshot comparison](branding-2026/comparison.html).

| Reference detail                            | Previous PWA                                          | Change                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inter headings, 600 weight, tight tracking  | Pixel display font for headings, callouts and numbers | Inter headings throughout, 24px section titles and responsive page titles; pixel identity stays in the wordmark |
| Neutral header and page; quiet card borders | Dark header, mint promotional block, more shadows     | Theme-aware neutral header, flatter cards, corrected page margins and 1200px content alignment                  |
| Illustrated monochrome hero                 | Plain dark gradient                                   | Original desktop and phone hero illustrations, locally cached                                                   |
| Eight colourful devroom patterns            | Text-only devroom selection                           | Original patterns on the home gallery and corresponding planning cards, keyed to 2026 programme tracks          |
| Clear section hierarchy and roomy links     | Small monospace navigation labels                     | Readable sans-serif navigation and labels, retained touch targets and visible choice controls                   |

The calendar, map and discovery remain app flows. Hero actions still open discovery
and Now; devroom links lead to whole-track preferences. The gold/green/grey choice
palette is retained. Archived and unknown tracks do not get unrelated 2026 artwork.

Artwork provenance is recorded in
[the asset README](../../apps/web/static/branding/2026/README.md).
Images and Inter ship locally; no font or banner CDN dependency is introduced.

## Validation

Browser inspection found no horizontal overflow on home, devrooms and discovery
at either captured size. New decorative banners loaded without errors. An
existing remote speaker portrait can remain unavailable; branding assets do not
depend on that host.

The 39 browser accessibility/design/discovery checks and 72 web unit tests passed.
An additional offline-artwork reload check verifies all eight cached banners.
Production build, typecheck and lint are checked separately; full CI is required
before merge. These screenshots are browser evidence, not physical-phone testing.
